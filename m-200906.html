…dear Daniel

your stomach starts to grind
the world becomes gray and dull place to live
and you begin to feel like
you can not bear it anymore
just come up
to the second floor
where the sun always shines
the birds sing and do nothing else
and the lion sleeps with the lamb
and eat away whatever you find
in our fridge…

And, voila!, the life will be fine again

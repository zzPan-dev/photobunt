die schmuggler haben ihre waren glücklich durchgebracht

Posted by: pezza pan in Blog, Curiosities

means: “The smugglers have fortunately smuggled their goods”.

This is a page from the most common Serbian-German dictionary. It was printed and reprinted for years and decades in Montenegro, and distributed all over former YU.

You can find here some very interesting examples for the words “durchbrechen”, “durchbrennen”, “durchbringen” and “durchdringen”.

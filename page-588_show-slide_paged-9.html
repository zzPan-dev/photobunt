crisensus bavaricus

Posted by: pezza pan in Blog

The Wolpertinger (Crisensus bavaricus) is a legendary creature supposedly living in the alpine forests of Bavaria in Germany. It has body parts of various animals — generally wings, antlers and fangs, all attached to the body of a small mammal. The most widespread description is that of a horned rabbit or horned squirrel.
